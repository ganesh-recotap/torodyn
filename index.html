Industrial buying doesn't look like a funnel. Most agencies still market as if it does.

The decision is made before sales hears about it

By the time an RFQ lands, a consultant has already written the specification and named the approved makes. If you aren't one of them, you're quoting on price.

A committee of engineers, not a single buyer

Design engineers, EPC project managers, plant heads, quality, procurement and finance all weigh in. Each needs a different kind of proof, and none of them want a sales pitch.

Cycles measured in quarters, not clicks

A switchgear or transformer order can take a year from enquiry to purchase order. Campaigns judged on 30-day lead counts miss almost everything that matters.

Torodyn is built around how orders actually move: we aim to put you on the approved makes list before the tender opens, and keep you there until the purchase order is signed.

How we work

Four stages. The first two take about six weeks and are fixed-fee, so you see our thinking before you commit to a retainer.

  1. 1

    Survey

    Weeks 1 to 3

    Interviews with your sales team, engineers and five customers. A win-loss review, and a map of accounts and competitors.

  2. 2

    Specify

    Weeks 3 to 6

    Positioning, messaging and a proof library: test reports, reference projects and calculators your buyers can check for themselves.

  3. 3

    Energise

    Month 2 onwards

    Campaigns to named accounts, content, events and channel programs, run alongside your sales team rather than handed over to it.

  4. 4

    Meter

    Every month

    A pipeline review with your sales head: specifications, RFQs, quotes and orders, account by account. We cut what isn't working.
